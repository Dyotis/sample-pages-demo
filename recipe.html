Classic Italian Carbonara

Ingredients

  • 400g spaghetti or rigatoni
  • 200g guanciale (or pancetta), cut into small strips
  • 4 large egg yolks + 2 whole eggs
  • 100g Pecorino Romano, finely grated
  • 50g Parmigiano Reggiano, finely grated
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • Salt for pasta water

Instructions

1Prepare the egg mixture. In a bowl, whisk together egg yolks, whole eggs, and most of the grated cheese. Add generous black pepper. Set aside.

2Cook the guanciale. In a large skillet over medium heat, cook guanciale strips until crispy and golden, about 8 minutes. Remove from heat but keep the rendered fat in the pan.

3Boil the pasta. Cook pasta in well-salted water until al dente. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water before draining.

4Combine everything. Add hot drained pasta to the skillet with guanciale (off heat). Toss to coat in the fat. Working quickly, pour the egg mixture over the pasta and toss vigorously. The residual heat will cook the eggs into a creamy sauce. Add pasta water a splash at a time if needed.

5Serve immediately. Plate and top with remaining cheese and more black pepper. Carbonara waits for no one!

Chef's Tip: The secret to perfect carbonara is temperature control. Never add the egg mixture to a hot pan on the stove — you'll get scrambled eggs. Remove the pan from heat first, then add the pasta, then the egg mixture.

Recipe adapted from Essentials of Italian Cooking by Marcella Hazan. Photography by Lisa Nguyen.
