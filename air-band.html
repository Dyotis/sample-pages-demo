Air Band

  1. Turn the phone sideways. Hold it with two hands.
  2. Tilt left or right to move to the next note.
  3. Jab the phone, or tap the screen, to play the note.
  4. Lay the phone flat for piano. Lift it half up for guitar. Hold it upright for drums.
Version 5. You hear a chord when the app starts. No chord? On iPhone, turn off silent mode. Then raise the volume.
